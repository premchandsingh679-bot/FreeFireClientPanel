## 🎮 How to Use

### Launch App
1. Find "Free Fire Client" in app drawer
2. Tap to open
3. Wait for splash screen (3 seconds)
4. Main dashboard appears

### Launch Floating Panel
1. Tap green "Launch Floating Panel" button
2. Grant overlay permission if prompted
3. Panel appears on screen
4. Drag to reposition

### Control Panel
- Toggle ES switch on/off
- Toggle AIM switch on/off
- Toggle FL switch on/off
- Click red ✕ to close panel
- Drag title bar to move

### Configure Settings
1. Tap blue "Settings" button
2. Toggle notifications on/off
3. Enable auto-launch if desired
4. View app info in About section
